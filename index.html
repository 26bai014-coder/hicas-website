About Us

Hindusthan College of Arts & Science (HICAS) was started in 1998 by the Hindusthan Educational & Charitable Trust. Our aim is to provide quality education for all students, irrespective of caste and creed. Our campus offers a peaceful setting, modern facilities and a good learning environment.
